02 · The growth case

Four ways Shopify Plus can create growth capacity.

The case is not “less custom.” It is putting Veneta’s custom advantage on top of a commerce core that can scale across markets, customers, and AI channels.

Related group proof

Australia has already solved the hardest technical question.

Swanky’s public implementation case shows the made-to-measure model can run on Shopify Plus.

744bnpossible combinations for one Honeycomb blind
>70%partner-reported conversion lift in under one month
Error-freefactory XML reported after launch, aside from human error
Automatedpricing, product creation, reporting, and factory feeds
Australia is a separate Nien Made sister company. Validate transferability, current performance, ownership, and reusable assets directly.
Protect

What already works

Direct-factory value, trust, rich product content, samples, remote help, at-home advice, and the recent NL/BE platform investment.

Unlock

Where growth can improve

Intent-led discovery, one customer/project identity, faster market rollout, consistent promises, AI-ready data, and more commercial self-service.

Decision posture

Benchmark—not rip and replace

Test the next market, AI journey, or adviser flow. Shopify earns a broader decision only through measurable economics.
